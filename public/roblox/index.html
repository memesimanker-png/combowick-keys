About Combo Wick

What is Combo Wick?

Combo Wick (also written ComboWick or Combo_WICK) is a Roblox script hub. It makes Lua scripts for Roblox games, runs a free key system and sells Premium keys at keys.combowick.com, and posts videos on YouTube (@Triple_WICK). It is not the Magic: The Gathering card "Wick, the Whorled Mind".

Is ComboWick the same as Combo_WICK?

Yes. ComboWick, Combo_WICK, COMBO_WICK and Combo Wick are the same Roblox script hub. The official sites are combowick.com and keys.combowick.com, and the Discord is discord.gg/ufrz9Zaqs8.

Are ComboWick scripts free?

Yes. Every script can be used with a free key (a few short ad steps). Some scripts also start as a free timed session with no key. Premium keys remove the ad steps and work in every supported game.
